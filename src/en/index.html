Fully online, via Telegram. Consultations are conducted as video calls. I work with clients from Russia, Europe, Israel, and other countries. All you need is a smartphone and the desire to change.

The minimum duration is 1 month. Most clients work with me for 3–6 months for sustainable results. First changes in how you feel within 2–3 weeks, visible physical changes within 4–8 weeks. Results are individual.

I work with adults without acute medical conditions. For chronic conditions, we'll discuss everything during the assessment. My consultations are coaching and educational support, not medical treatment. For serious conditions, I work alongside your doctor, not instead of them.

I accept payment in EUR, USD, and RUB. Payment is made via online transfer after the first free session. Details via direct message on Telegram.

Most approaches deal with food or workouts separately. I work with both, plus lifestyle: sleep, stress, daily routine. The program is built around your real life, not an ideal schedule. That's why it works long-term.
